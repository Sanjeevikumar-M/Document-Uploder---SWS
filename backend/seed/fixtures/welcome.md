# Welcome to Document Uploader

Welcome to the Document Uploader system.

## Overview
This platform allows team members to upload, manage, and query internal documents such as policies, project briefs, and notes.

## Key Features
- **Upload:** Drag and drop or browse `.txt`, `.md`, or `.json` files.
- **Manage:** View stored documents, download original files, or remove obsolete files.
- **AI Chatbot:** Ask questions about any uploaded document and get relevant answers with source citations.

## Tips
- Keep documents structured with clear headings.
- Check the Expense Policy or Project Brief for quick answers.
