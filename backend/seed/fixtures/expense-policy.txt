ACME CORP – EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (v3)

1. Eligibility
   All full-time employees may claim work-related expenses. Contractors must have prior written approval from their engagement manager.

2. Meals
   Daily meal allowance is 40 USD for domestic travel and 65 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

3. Travel
   Economy airfare only for flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more with manager approval.

4. Submission
   Submit receipts within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days will not be reimbursed. Attach a scanned or photographed receipt for any expense over 25 USD.

5. Approval
   Expenses under 500 USD are approved by your line manager. Expenses of 500 USD or more require director-level approval.
