PROJECT BRIEF – ORION ONBOARDING REVAMP

Goal
  Cut new-hire time-to-productivity from 4 weeks to 2 weeks.

Scope
  - Redesign the first-day checklist.
  - Automate laptop provisioning and account creation.
  - Build a self-serve knowledge base for the top 20 support questions.

Timeline
  Kickoff: March 3. Beta with one team: April 14. Company-wide: May 26.

Owners
  Product: Priya Raman. Engineering: Sam Okafor. People Ops: Lena Fischer.

Success metrics
  - Median time-to-first-commit under 5 business days.
  - New-hire satisfaction score of 4.5/5 or higher.
  - Fewer than 2 provisioning tickets per new hire.
